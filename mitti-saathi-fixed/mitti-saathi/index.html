About Mitti Saathi

Mitti Saathi is a farming information website. It lets farmers choose their State, District and Taluka and see the current weather and a 7-day forecast for that taluka, including temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind speed and wind direction.

Weather data is retrieved from Open-Meteo using the approximate coordinates of each taluka's headquarters town. The values therefore describe conditions near that town, not a measurement at a specific field.
